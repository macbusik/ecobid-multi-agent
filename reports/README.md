## Purpose

Reports are kept separate from `.kiro/specs/` to maintain a clean separation between:
- **Specs** (`.kiro/specs/`) - Requirements, design, and tasks that drive development
- **Reports** (`reports/`) - Analysis, metrics, and documentation generated from the system

## Guidelines

- Reports should be dated in the filename or header
- Update existing reports rather than creating new versions
- Archive old reports if they become obsolete
